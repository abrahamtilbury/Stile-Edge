WHAT IS AN APRON?

More depth. More presence.

Apron edging creates a deeper finished edge by combining the primary floor tile with additional tile material. The result is a substantial architectural finish that can be used where a standard profiled tile does not provide enough depth.

Built From Your Tile

Matching tile material can be used to create a deeper edge while maintaining continuity with the surrounding surface.

Designed For Coping

Apron profiles are particularly suited to pool coping and applications where a fuller, more substantial edge is required.

Multiple Finishes

Choose from a precision mitred junction, a rounded edge or a contemporary flat bevelled finish.

APRON PROFILES

Three ways to finish the edge.

Each apron profile is manufactured around the tile you have selected, allowing the finished edge to become part of the tile rather than an additional trim product.

01

PRECISION JOINT

Mitred Apron

A precision-machined solution for situations where two tiled surfaces need to meet at a right angle.

Each piece is machined to create a clean 45° mitred junction, producing a continuous architectural appearance without relying on hand-mitred tile edges being prepared on site.

02

ROUNDED

Round Edge Apron

A deeper rounded edge designed to give pool coping a fuller, more substantial appearance while retaining the soft visual character of a rounded profile.

A strip of matching tile is bonded at right angles beneath the primary tile. The front edge is then machined into Stile Edge's popular rounded profile.

CONTEMPORARY

Flat Edge With Bevel Apron

A squared architectural edge for those wanting a contemporary alternative to the traditional rounded bullnose finish.

An additional piece of matching tile is bonded beneath the primary tile to create the deeper construction. The front face is machined flat before the upper and lower edges are softened with a small bevel.

APRON CONSTRUCTION

Designed around the finished edge.

Round Edge and Flat Edge With Bevel apron profiles use additional tile material to create greater depth at the finished edge.

The additional tile strip is bonded at right angles to the primary floor tile, creating a substantial edge while allowing the finished surface to remain visually consistent with the surrounding tile.

INSTALLATION CONSIDERATIONS

Built for strength as well as appearance.

Matching Tile Strip

Round Edge and Flat Edge With Bevel aprons can be constructed using a matching tile strip, typically around 30–90mm in height depending on the application and desired appearance.

Full Support

When installing apron coping, the apron strip should be fully supported with tile adhesive to achieve maximum strength and durability.

Project Specific

The finished construction can vary depending on the tile, application, dimensions and desired appearance. Talk to the Stile Edge team before ordering for project-specific advice.
