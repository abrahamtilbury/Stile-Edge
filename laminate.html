THE LAMINATE PROCESS

Built from two pieces of tile.

Laminate edging creates a thicker, more substantial finished edge by joining an additional piece of matching tile beneath the primary tile.

The additional tile is carefully cut, positioned and bonded to the underside of the tile edge. Once the adhesive has fully cured, the combined piece can be machined into the required profile.

This allows the finished edge to have significantly more visual depth than a standard single-piece profile.

01

Cut

A matching strip of tile is prepared to form the additional edge material.

02

Laminate

The additional tile is bonded to the underside of the main tile.

03

Cure

The adhesive is allowed to fully cure before further machining.

04

Profile

The laminated tile is then machined into the selected finished profile.

LAMINATED PROFILES

Choose the edge that suits the project.

01

FULLER · TRADITIONAL

Laminated Full Bullnose

A chunkier, more prominent bullnose for projects that call for a thicker finished edge.

The Laminated Full Bullnose is created by cutting a strip of matching tile and laminating it to the underside of the main tile.

Once the adhesive has fully dried, the combined tile is put through the bullnosing process and honed or polished as required.

The result is a fuller, more traditional profile with a stronger visual presence than a standard Full Bullnose.

Full bullnose Double thickness Pool coping Fuller edge
LEAD TIME

Due to the additional fabrication involved, Laminated Full Bullnose profiles are more labour intensive and may require longer lead times.

02

ROUNDED · CONTEMPORARY

Laminate Round Edge

A Half Bullnose with additional tile beneath the edge for a thicker, more substantial appearance.

The Laminate Round Edge combines the familiar rounded appearance of a Half Bullnose with an additional piece of matching tile underneath.

This creates the appearance of a thicker tile while maintaining a clean, contemporary finish.

The result is a classic rounded top with a square lower section, creating an unobtrusive architectural edge.

Half bullnose Laminated underside Rounded top Pool coping
03

CONTEMPORARY · SQUARED

Laminated Flat Edge With Bevel

A chunky square finish for those moving away from the traditional rounded bullnose.

An additional piece of matching tile is bonded to the underside of the primary tile to create a double-thickness edge.

The front face is then machined to produce a perfectly flat finish. Finally, a small arras is cut along the top and bottom edges.

This produces a contemporary, unobtrusive edge with softened transitions rather than the rounded appearance of a bullnose.

Flat face Bevelled edge Double thickness Pool coping Stair treads
