STANDARD PROFILES

The finished edge makes the tile.

We take virtually any suitable floor tile and machine the edge into a finished profile for pool coping, stair nosing, skirting, patios, balconies and more.

CUSTOM TILE PROFILING

One tile.
Multiple possibilities.

A suitable floor tile can be transformed into a finished architectural edge without relying on a separate edging product.

From rounded pool coping to contemporary square edges and integrated stair tread grooves, our profiles are machined directly into the tile.

01

MOST POPULAR

Half Bullnose

A versatile rounded edge profile suited to almost any application.

The Half Bullnose is one of Stile Edge's most popular edging profiles because of its versatility and clean finished appearance.

It is particularly suited to pool coping, stair nosings, skirting tiles, sill tiles, patios and balconies.

The profile can be applied to a wide range of suitable tiles, including many fully vitrified and glazed tiles.

Pool coping Stair nosing Skirting Sill tiles Patios Balconies
02

ROUNDED

Full Bullnose

A fully rounded edge for a soft, uncluttered finish.

The Full Bullnose creates a completely rounded finished edge, softening the appearance of the tile while maintaining a clean and minimal aesthetic.

Around swimming pools, the profile can create a subtle rounded lip or overhang, providing a simple and unobtrusive finished edge.

Pool coping Rounded edge Soft finish Overhang
03

CONTEMPORARY

Flat Edge With Bevel

A clean squared profile with softened edges.

The Flat Edge With Bevel is designed for projects where a contemporary, squared appearance is preferred over a rounded bullnose.

The machined flat face creates a simple architectural edge while the bevel softens the exposed corners.

It is particularly suited to modern pool surrounds and other architectural applications.

Square edge Pool coping Contemporary Architectural
04

SUBTLE FINISH

Pencil Round

A restrained contemporary edge with a gentle rounded finish.

The Pencil Round is a simple profile designed to soften exposed tile edges without creating the more pronounced shape of a bullnose.

It is well suited to copings, steps and other applications where a subtle rounded edge is preferred.

The rounded edge can also help reduce exposed sharp corners and provide a more understated finished appearance.

Coping Steps Softened edge Contemporary
05

STAIR TREAD

Step Tread Grooves

Integrated tread grooves machined directly into the tile.

Step Tread Grooves provide an alternative to adding a separate stair nosing product. Instead, grooves are machined directly into the selected floor tile.

Sets of three, four or five grooves can be produced, with the grooves machined at approximately 6mm wide.

This creates a dedicated non-slip section within the tile itself while allowing the stair tread to match the surrounding tile.

3 grooves 4 grooves 5 grooves 6mm groove width
Tested performance

Stile Edge states that Step Tread Grooves achieved a “V” rating, approximately equivalent to R12, under AS/NZS 4586:2004 testing described on its current website.
