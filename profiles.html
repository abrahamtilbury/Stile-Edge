Custom Tile Edging Profiles

We machine the edges of your tiles into finished profiles for pool coping, stair nosing, skirting and architectural applications.

Standard Tile Profiles

Our standard profiles are designed to cover the most common edging requirements, providing clean, consistent finishes for everyday tiling applications.

01 Half Bullnose

Our most popular profile. A simple rounded edge that is economical and quick to produce. Ideal for stair nosing, skirting and pool coping where a clean, practical finish is needed.

Pool coping Stair nosing Skirting

02 Full Bullnose

A fully rounded edge that softens the tile and creates a small overhang. A good choice when you want a more substantial soft edge on pool surrounds or other exposed edges.

Pool coping Soft edge Overhang

03 Flat Edge Bevel

A clean squared profile with a bevelled edge. Suits contemporary pool surrounds and architectural work where a sharper, more defined look is preferred.

Square edge Pool coping Contemporary

04 Pencil Round

A restrained contemporary profile that softens the edge without adding bulk. Useful on steps, coping and any situation where you want the tile to look finished but not heavy.

Coping Steps Softened edge

Apron Profiles

Deeper finished edges for pool coping, stair nosing and other exposed edges. Choose a mitred finish or a laminated apron construction.

05 Mitred Apron

A clean 45° mitre where two tiled surfaces meet. Pre-machined in the factory so tradespeople avoid cutting and finishing each edge on site.

Corners Junctions Commercial

06 Flat Edge Bevel Apron

A squared architectural edge with a bevelled face, designed for a clean contemporary coping finish.

Flat face Bevelled edge Architectural

Step & Safety Profiles

Non-slip tread options cut directly into the tile for stairs and wet areas.

07 Step Tread Grooves

Grooves cut directly into the tile surface to create an integrated non-slip tread.

Pools Stairs Wet Areas
