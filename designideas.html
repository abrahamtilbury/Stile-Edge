Design Ideas for tile edges

Custom edge profiles that give designers and architects more control over the finished look of pools, stairs, patios and architectural details.

Edge profiles that support the design, not limit it.

We machine the edge of the tile you specify so the finished detail matches the intent of the project.

Whether you need a soft minimalist edge, a classic full bullnose, or a thicker laminated profile for architectural presence, the edge is produced to suit the application.

Where the edge matters

01

Pool Coping

Half bullnose and full bullnose remain the most common choices. Flat edge bevel and laminated aprons are increasingly used on contemporary Queensland pool projects.

02

Stair Nosing

Half bullnose for a clean practical finish, or step tread grooves when an integrated non-slip surface is required without a separate nosing product.

03

Skirting & Borders

Pencil round or half bullnose give a finished look to skirting and border tiles without adding bulk.

04

Architectural Edges

Laminated profiles create a thicker, more substantial edge. Useful for feature sills, deep coping and situations where a mitred join needs greater strength.

Design flexibility

Available profiles include half bullnose, full bullnose, flat edge bevel, pencil round, laminated edges and apron constructions.

Softened edges suit minimalist projects. Larger bullnose profiles give a more classic or substantial look. Laminated edges deliver the chunky architectural finish some designs require.

This type of profile is becoming increasingly popular with Queensland pool builders offering up-to-date designs.

Current directions

Many current projects favour cleaner, more restrained edges. Pencil round and flat edge bevel are frequently specified for contemporary work.

At the same time, laminated and apron profiles continue to be used where greater depth or a stronger visual presence is needed at the finished edge.

If you need samples or CAD drawings for a specific profile, contact us with the tile and application details.
