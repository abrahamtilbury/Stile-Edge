Design Ideas for tile edges

How different edge profiles shape the finished look of pools, stairs, bathrooms and architectural details.

The edge is part of the design.

We machine the edge of the tile you specify so the finished detail matches the intent of the project — from soft minimalist lines to deeper architectural edges.

01

Pool Coping

Half bullnose and full bullnose remain the most common choices for pool surrounds. Flat edge bevel and mitred or laminated aprons are increasingly used on contemporary Queensland pool projects where a cleaner or deeper edge is preferred.

Half Bullnose Full Bullnose Mitred Apron Flat Edge Bevel
02

Stairs & Step Treads

Half bullnose gives a clean practical nosing. Step tread grooves cut directly into the tile create an integrated non-slip surface without a separate nosing product — useful for external stairs and wet areas.

Half Bullnose Step Tread Grooves Wet Areas
03

Bathrooms

Pencil round and half bullnose soften exposed edges on benches, niches and wall returns. Higher-end bathroom work increasingly uses mitred edges for a clean junction where two tiled surfaces meet.

Pencil Round Half Bullnose Mitred Edges
04

Skirting & Borders

A simple rounded or bevelled edge gives skirting and border tiles a finished look without adding bulk. Half bullnose and pencil round are the usual choices here.

Half Bullnose Pencil Round Flat Edge Bevel
05

Architectural Edges

Laminated profiles combine two pieces of tile to create a thicker, more substantial edge. Useful for feature sills, deep coping and situations where a mitred join needs greater visual weight.

Laminated Deep Coping Feature Sills
