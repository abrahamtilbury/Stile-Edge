The edge is everything.

Stile Edge has been profiling and custom edging tiles on the Gold Coast for over 15 years, supplying tile retailers, builders and trade customers across South East Queensland and beyond.

We specialise in custom tile edging and profiling.

Stile Edge specialises in profiling and custom edging of tiles for pool coping, stair nosing, skirting, sills and other exposed edges.

Based in Currumbin Waters on the Gold Coast, we work with tile retailers, builders and tilers across South East Queensland, with work extending as far north as Mackay and west to Toowoomba.

Our own factory.
Controlled from start to finish.

All profiling is done in our dedicated Currumbin Waters factory. Tiles are cut using water-cooled machinery to control dust during the cutting process.

Cutting and gluing are carried out in separate areas of the factory. Before bonding, tiles are cleaned and dried for consistent adhesion.

From cutting and profiling through to gluing, packing and handling, quality control checks are performed before each job leaves the factory.

Our facility is powered by an off-grid solar system, helping supply its day-to-day operation.

Experience behind the edge.

Our team works with tilers, builders and suppliers to help choose the best profile for the job, taking into account the design, application and budget.

For larger or more detailed projects, we can also arrange site inspections to help establish the requirements before production begins.

From smaller custom jobs to larger commercial projects, we work with customers across a range of project sizes.

Show us the tile. We'll help with the edge.

Send us the tile details, dimensions, quantity and project requirements and we'll help determine the most suitable profile for the job.
