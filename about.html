The edge is everything.

Custom tile profiling and edging for pool coping, stair nosing, skirting and architectural finishes.

Gold Coast, Queensland Custom Tile Profiling

Turning ordinary tiles into finished architectural edges.

Stile Edge specialises in custom tile edging and profiling, giving designers, tile suppliers, builders and tilers a way to create finished edges from the tile itself.

Rather than relying on separate edging products, a suitable tile can be machined into a purpose-made edge for the application.

From pool coping and stair nosing through to skirting, sill tiles, patios and balconies, the finished edge becomes part of the tile rather than an afterthought.

Your tile. Our craft.

The idea behind Stile Edge is simple: take the tile you've chosen for your project and create the edge you actually want.

This gives the finished project a more integrated appearance while allowing the tile itself to remain the visual feature.

A finished edge without the add-on.

Traditional edging products can limit the choice of tile or introduce another material into an otherwise continuous design. Profiling the tile itself creates another option.

The profile changes the character of the tile.

A rounded edge can soften a pool surround. A bevel can introduce a sharper architectural detail. A tread groove can create a functional finish without introducing a separate nosing product.

Stile Edge offers a range of standard profiles as well as more substantial laminate and apron constructions.

Detail matters at the edge.

The edge is often one of the most visible details in a tiled project. It is where separate surfaces meet, where light catches the material, and where the quality of the finish becomes obvious.

That's why the profiling process is about more than simply removing material from a tile. The finished profile needs to complement the tile, the application and the overall design.

Stile Edge works with tile suppliers, tilers, builders, designers and project teams to provide custom edging suited to their requirements.
