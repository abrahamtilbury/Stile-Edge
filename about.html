Gold Coast, Queensland Custom Tile Profiling

We specialise in exceptional quality custom tile edging and profiling.

Stile Edge specialises in profiling and custom edging of tiles for pool coping, stair nosing, skirting, sills and other exposed edges.

We supply tile retailers and trade customers across South East Queensland, giving customers more options when a standard edging tile isn't the right fit.

Detail matters at the edge.

Pool builders and designers love our profiling services and seperate your tiles from traditional tile offerings.
