THE STILE EDGE DIFFERENCE

Your Tile. Our Craft.

We are able to take virtually any suitable tile and profile the edge to transform it into beautiful pool coping, stair nosing, skirting or border tile.

Why Tile Edging?

Having your tiles edged eliminates the need for additional finishing products and creates a clean, integrated result.

All Types of Tiles
