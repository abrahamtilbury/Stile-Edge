Your Tile. Our Finish.

We take your tiles and profile the edge to create pool coping, stair nosing, skirting and border tiles for your tiling project.

01

Custom Profiles

Stile Edge machines the tile edge with a profile that best suits your design requirements.

02

Manufactured Locally

Your tiles are profiled locally using water-cooled machinery to control dust during cutting. Bonding is carried out in a separate, controlled area of the factory.

03

Expert Advice

Our team can help you choose a profile based on the tile, application and budget.

04

Built for trade

From single-room renovations to large commercial projects, we have the capacity and experience to handle trade orders of all sizes.
