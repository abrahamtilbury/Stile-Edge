APRON PROFILES

When the edge needs more depth.

Apron construction creates a deeper, more substantial finished edge for pool coping and architectural applications.

LAMINATE PROFILES

A fuller, architectural edge.

Laminated profiles use additional tile material beneath the primary tile to create greater thickness and visual depth.

The result is a substantial finished edge while maintaining the appearance of a tile-based solution.
